Sharing information about your health conditions


The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP), or approved healthcare professionals who work for DWP, may need more information about:

  • your health conditions and how they affect you
  • medication you have been prescribed

We may get this information from your:

  • health and care professionals that you tell us about
  • your NHS record

How we uses this information

We will only ask or look for information relevant to your claim. We use this information to:

  • process your claim
  • make a decision on your claim, or any mandatory reconsideration or appeal you make

The law allows us to obtain, keep and use this information.

We ask for your consent on behalf of the NHS, your doctor, or other relevant professionals you tell them about. This reassures the NHS, your doctor and the others that they can legally share this information.

We can lawfully ask your doctor, hospital, or other relevant professionals for information about your health condition and how it affects you. This is because we are asking for information to help us carry out our official security functions.

You do not have to give consent. If you do not, we will make a decision based on the information we have and any additional information you provide.

Do you give consent for your doctor or other relevant professionals to give DWP more information about your health condition?


Do you give consent for DWP, or approved healthcare professionals, to access your NHS record to view relevant information about your health conditions and medication?

If you change your mind

You can change your mind and give or withdraw your consent at any time.

Call the PIP enquiry line and say you want to give or withdraw your consent to DWP accessing your NHS record.

If you do withdraw your consent after giving it, we will no longer be allowed to:

  • ask your health and care professionals to share information
  • view your NHS record
Enquiry line number: 0800 121 4433
