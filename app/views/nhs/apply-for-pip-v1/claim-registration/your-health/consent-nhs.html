Sharing information from your NHS record


The Department for Work and Pensions (DWP), or approved healthcare professionals that work for them, may need more information about:

  • your health conditions and how they affect you
  • medication you have been prescribed

DWP may access your NHS record to view this information.

How DWP uses this information

DWP will only use information relevant to your claim. DWP use this information to:

  • process your claim
  • make a decision on your claim, or any mandatory reconsideration or appeal you make

The law allows them to get, keep and use this information.

You do not have to give your consent. If you do not, DWP will make a decision based on the information we have and any additional information you provide.

Do you give consent for DWP, or approved healthcare professionals, to access your NHS record to view relevant information about your health conditions and medication?

If you change your mind

You can change your mind and give or withdraw your consent at any time.

Call the PIP enquiry line and say you want to give or withdraw your consent to DWP accessing your NHS record.

If you do withdraw your consent after previously giving it, DWP will no longer be allowed to view your NHS record.

Enquiry line number: 0800 121 4433
